# Angel Mermaid Tide

A multi-dimensional VR / animated film concept exploring redemption, memory, release, and awakening.

Angel Mermaid Tide is a spiritual storyworld centered on Ursula’s redemption, anchored by conscience, deep sleep, and the tide of release. A dreamlike journey through love, memory, and transformation, where the angel-mermaid becomes a vessel for forgiveness, surrender, and awakening.

## Concept
This project imagines a layered reality where memory, dream, and spirit intersect. Ursula’s journey unfolds across dreamlike dimensions, blending emotional truth with mystical transformation. The tide becomes both a literal and symbolic force — carrying release, remembrance, and renewal.

- Conscience
- Deep sleep
- Release
- Transformation
- Love
- Spiritual awakening

## Medium
- VR experience

- Multi-dimensional narrative world

## Tone
Luminous, dreamlike, spiritual, cinematic, emotional, and mythic.

## Vision
A story of awakening through surrender — where love and conscience guide the soul through submerged memory and into a new form of freedom.
