# Project brief

## Concept
Angel Mermaid Tide is a multi-dimensional spiritual storyworld centered on Ursula’s redemption, anchored by conscience, deep sleep, and the tide of release.

## Core idea
This project imagines a dreamlike reality where memory, spirit, and transformation intersect. Ursula journeys through layered dimensions shaped by love, surrender, forgiveness, and awakening.

## Medium
- VR experience
- animated film concept
- cinematic worldbuilding
- atmospheric storytelling

## Tone
- luminous
- spiritual
- dreamlike
- mythic
- cinematic
- emotionally resonant

## Visual direction
- deep space palette
- obsidian, navy, cyan, pearl, moonlit tones
- celestial motion and subtle glow
- symbolic, layered composition
- immersive atmospheric design

## Audience
This project is meant to feel like a cinematic artistic experience rather than a conventional product landing page. The audience is likely drawn to immersive storytelling, spiritual worldbuilding, and visual mood.

## Creative intent
The project should feel like a threshold between memory and transformation. The angel-mermaid is not simply a figure but a vessel of release, awakened through conscience, love, and surrender.

## What to protect
- the spiritual tone
- the emotional depth
- the concept-driven narrative
- the mythic atmosphere
- the sense of layered reality

## What not to do
- do not flatten the project into a generic business landing page
- do not remove the dreamlike symbolism
- do not introduce overly literal or mechanical language
- do not dilute the emotional and mystical tone
